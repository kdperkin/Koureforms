What we do

Koure Forms works at both ends of a project. On screen, we draw the plan and model it in 3D. On site, we craft it. Buy the plans and craft it yourself, or have us craft it.

How we work

Every project follows the same five steps, whether you want plans or a finished craft.

  1. Vision

    Talk through what you want to craft and where.

  2. Design

    Create the blueprint and the 3D model.

  3. Review

    Walk through the design together and make revisions.

  4. Craft or hand off

    We craft it, or deliver DIY-ready plans.

  5. Finish

    Final walkthrough and photos for the portfolio.

About the company

Koure Forms is founder-led and hands-on. The owner brings field experience to every design: framing decks, crafting yurt platforms and large outdoor event structures, working with lumber in the warehouse and operating heavy equipment. That jobsite experience is why Koure Forms plans are buildable.

  • Designer and builder in one. The person drawing the plan knows how it gets crafted.
  • See it before you craft it. 3D designs reduce surprises and wasted material.
  • Crafted to last. Structural thinking comes first.
  • Flexible. Buy the plans and DIY, or have us craft it.
